Privacy policy

Draft: have this checked before you launch.

BabyVibes is a trading name of Hostdigi (Pty) Ltd, the responsible party for your personal information. We respect your privacy and handle your personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you order, we collect your name, email address, cellphone number and delivery address. If you create an account, we also keep your saved address, your order history and your password (stored encrypted, so nobody, including us, can read it). Payment details are entered on Yoco's secure page and never reach us.

Why we use it

We use your details only to process, deliver and support your order, and to send you order and delivery updates. We'll only send you marketing if you ask us to, and you can opt out at any time.

Reviews

If you leave a review, we publish only the name and town you choose, with your ratings and comments. We never publish your email or order number.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with our payment provider (Yoco), our email provider (Brevo, which sends your order and account emails) and our courier to complete your order. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting purposes, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can update your details on the My account page at any time. You may also ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by emailing {{email}}. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Cookies and storage

Your cart and checkout details are saved in your own browser so they're there when you come back, and a session cookie keeps you logged in to your account.

With your permission, we also use Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager) to understand how people find and use our shop, for example which pages are visited and which products are added to carts, and to measure our advertising. We also use Hotjar (by Contentsquare) to record anonymous visits and make heatmaps, so we can see where the shop is confusing and fix it. Recordings never include what you type into forms: names, addresses, phone numbers, emails and passwords are hidden. These cookies and recordings are only used if you click Accept on the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. Google's use of this data is described in its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).
