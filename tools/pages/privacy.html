Privacy policy

Draft: have this checked before you launch.

We respect your privacy and handle your personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you order, we collect your name, email address, cellphone number and delivery address. Payment details are entered on Yoco's secure page and never reach us.

Why we use it

We use your details only to process, deliver and support your order, and to send you order and delivery updates. We'll only send you marketing if you ask us to, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with our payment provider (Yoco) and our courier to complete your order. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting purposes, and then delete them.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by emailing {{email}}. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Cookies and storage

Your cart and checkout details are saved in your own browser so they're there when you come back. We don't use advertising cookies.
